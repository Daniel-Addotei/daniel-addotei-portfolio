About

Where science meets systems.

My work sits at the intersection of epidemiology, surveillance intelligence, health systems, laboratory-informed public health and emergency operations.

I am an epidemiologist and public health practitioner based in Accra, Ghana. My professional journey began in biomedical laboratory science and has expanded through field epidemiology, outbreak investigation, surveillance, public health informatics and emergency management.

I am particularly interested in how countries can connect surveillance, laboratory, operational and One Health data to detect threats earlier and support better decisions. Alongside applied public health work, I contribute to research, technical guidance, curriculum development and workforce capacity building.
